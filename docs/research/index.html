Research

Research philosophy

Space-Ready Navigation Technology.

From low Earth orbit to deep space, I develop navigation technologies that can be implemented, verified, and demonstrated in real mission environments.

Hardware-in-the-Loop Simulation (HILS) connects theory to flight hardware, advancing reliable navigation systems from early concepts toward mission deployment and commercial application.
