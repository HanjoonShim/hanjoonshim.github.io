    Farewell and New Beginning

    October 01, 2025 · News

    From March 2017 to September 2025, I have been part of the GNSS Laboratory at Seoul National University, completing my graduate studies and concluding my journey as a postdoctoral researcher.

    I am deeply grateful to Prof. Changdon Kee, whose pioneering Ph.D. work on Wide-Area DGPS (WADGPS) laid the groundwork for what became today’s Satellite-Based Augmentation Systems (SBAS). His invaluable guidance has shaped my growth as a researcher, and I also extend my sincere thanks to all my colleagues for their companionship and support throughout these years.

    In particular, I wish for the successful launch of SNUGLITE-III CubeSat scheduled on November 27, 2025. 🚀

    This October, I begin my new chapter as a Postdoctoral Researcher in Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering at the University of Liverpool, joining Dr Stefania Soldini’s research group. My research will focus on Guidance, Navigation, and Control (GNC) for asteroid missions under the #REMORA project.

    I look forward to this new stage of life, and I hope to continue building meaningful collaborations at future conferences and beyond.
